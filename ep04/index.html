just in case

/ˌdʒʌst ɪn ˈkeɪs/ phrase

Used when you prepare for something that might happen.

From the episodeMiaScene 7

Yes, just in case it gets cold!

Then pause and say it out loud.

Use it in real life

  • Take an umbrella just in case it rains.

    What it means: Take it because it might rain.

  • I always keep some snacks in the car, just in case.

    What it means: I keep them there in case I need them later.
