rescue

/ˈreskjuː/ noun

Help given to save someone or something from danger.

From the episodeNarratorScene 7

A large rescue truck drove slowly through the storm, carrying very precious cargo.

Then pause and say it out loud.

Use it in real life

  • The rescue team found the lost hikers.

    What it means: The team that helps people in danger found them.

  • We adopted our cat from an animal rescue.

    What it means: A place that saves animals gave us our cat.
