pen pal

/ˈpen pæl/ noun

A friend you write letters to, even though they live far away.

From the episodeNarratorScene 9

A pen pal is a friend you write letters to, even when they live far, far away — and today, Leo was about to meet his.

Then pause and say it out loud.

Use it in real life

  • My pen pal lives in Brazil. We write every month.

    What it means: A friend far away who I send letters to.

  • Do you have a pen pal in another country?

    What it means: A friend abroad you write to.
