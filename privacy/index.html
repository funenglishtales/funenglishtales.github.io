Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Who we are

Fun English Tales is a YouTube channel that helps people learn English through stories about the Thompson family. This policy covers two things: the channel's internal tool (the Google Cloud app "HappyDollars Youtube") and the Word Practice pages on this website.

The internal tool ("HappyDollars Youtube")

  • It is a private tool for the Fun English Tales channel. Only the channel owner uses it. It is not offered to the public.
  • It only accesses the channel owner's own Google Drive and Google Sheets. It uses them to save production reports and production logs.
  • It does not access, collect, sell or share data about anyone else.
  • All data stays in the channel owner's own Google Drive and Google Sheets. We do not copy it to other servers.
  • The channel owner can remove the tool's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The Word Practice pages

  • The pages do not collect personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-up forms and no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • When you tick a word as "practised", that choice is saved only in your own browser (local storage) so it is still there next time. It is never sent to us. You can clear it by clearing your browser's site data.
  • The pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website host, these services may see basic technical details such as your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Links to our videos open YouTube, where YouTube's privacy policy applies.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
