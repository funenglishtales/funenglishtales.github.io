anniversary

/ˌænɪˈvɜːrsəri/ noun

The same date every year when something special happened, like a wedding.

From the episodeNarratorScene 8

Today was their wedding anniversary.

Then pause and say it out loud.

Use it in real life

  • My parents' wedding anniversary is in June.

    What it means: They got married in June, so they celebrate every June.

  • We went out for dinner on our fifth anniversary.

    What it means: It was five years since that special day.
